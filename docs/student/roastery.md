# You own: Roastery

**Your folder:** `sam/roastery/` · read [GROUND-RULES.md](./GROUND-RULES.md) first.

You are the middle of the chain — the physical transformation from raw beans
into something that can be sold. A roastery has one machine at the center of
it, a rotating drum, and everything you own hangs off one question: what goes
into it next, and what comes out?

Some products are one bean, roasted alone. Some are several beans, weighed
out and roasted *together* as a single batch. And some are two coffees that
have to be roasted **separately** and combined afterward — because they need
different amounts of heat, and one drum can't produce two roast levels at
once.

## Your responsibilities as a team

- Turn green coffee into roasted coffee: charge a machine, run it, record
  what came out.
- Respect that a machine has both a maximum batch size and a meaningful
  minimum — too little coffee in the drum ruins the roast just as surely as
  too much.
- Handle products where the job isn't actually finished until more than one
  batch has completed.
- Decide, on any given day, what's actually worth roasting, and on which
  machine.

## Words you need

- **Roastable** — one drumful: either a single bean, or several beans weighed
  out to be roasted together.
- **Charge / drop** — the weight going into the drum, and the weight coming
  out. They're never the same number.
- **Roast level** — how far a batch was taken. Darker takes longer and loses
  more weight.
- **Resting** — freshly roasted coffee can't ship immediately. Combining two
  already-roasted coffees afterward doesn't change that.
- **Roast day / carryover** — you don't roast per order; you gather demand and
  roast on a schedule, and there's always some coffee left in the bins
  afterward that has to be accounted for tomorrow.

## Events to think about

- Two coffees roasted separately need to be combined into one product later.
  How would you keep track of a job that isn't done until two separate things
  have both happened?
- If you combine two already-roasted coffees into one product, does the
  result get to ship right away — or does something about each of the
  originals still apply to it?
- Every roast day leaves some coffee sitting unused in the bins. What should
  happen to it tomorrow, and who needs to know it's there?
- Somebody wants to know how much coffee is in your bins right now, without
  asking you directly. What would you have to publish for that to be
  possible?

## Before you write any code

Write a one-line charter on an index card — what you're called, what you
listen for, what you send — and post it on the board in your context's area.
Then mark the board: one color of dot on the events you'll publish, another on
the events you'll listen for. Next, find the Inventory & Packaging team and
agree on who owns roasted bulk coffee once it exists — you, until it's bagged,
or them, the moment it comes out of the drum.
